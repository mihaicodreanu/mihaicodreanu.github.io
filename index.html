About Me

I work in the Econ of AI research team at Google. This website mostly reflects work before/during my PhD and reflects only my personal views.

I have an Economics PhD from Stanford and an MPhil in Economics from Oxford (Nuffield College, Clarendon Scholar).

When I'm not debugging code, you can find me playing racket sports, board games or traveling.

You can email me anytime at: mihaicod@google.com or mihaic@stanford.edu.
